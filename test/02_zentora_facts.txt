Zentora Labs was founded in 2019 by Linh Tran and is headquartered in Da Nang, Vietnam.
The company has 48 employees across three offices: Da Nang, Ho Chi Minh City, and Singapore.
Zentora's main product is called QuillBase, a note-taking app with built-in encryption.
QuillBase version 3.2 was released on 14 August 2026 and added offline mode.
The annual company retreat takes place every October on Phu Quoc island.
Office hours are 9:00 to 17:30, Monday to Friday. The Singapore office closes at 18:00.
