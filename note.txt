2. Ask these (POST /chat)

Use "session_id": "s1" for all of them:

Question	Expected answer
What embedding model does MindSync use?	all-MiniLM-L6-v2
How often does MindSync back up to Filecoin?	Every 30 minutes
What is the chunk size?	256 tokens, with 32 overlap
When is the weekly review meeting?	Friday at 9am
What time does Northwind Bakery close?	6pm (closed Sundays)
How much is a cardamom bun?	3.50 dollars
Who founded the bakery?	Elena Park

Extra tests

Follow-up (memory): after “What is the chunk size?”, ask And how much do they overlap? in the same session. This only works if the chat remembers the previous turn.
Unanswerable: ask What is the capital of France? or Who is the CEO of Northwind Bakery?. A good setup says it doesn’t know. A 0.5B model may answer anyway, which tells you how much to trust it.
Multi-document: ask Which one is closed on Sundays?, which should pick the bakery.

Check the sources field in each response. For the MindSync questions it should list mindsync-notes, and for the bakery questions bakery-menu. If the sources are right but the answer is wrong, the retrieval is working and the LLM is the weak part. In that case, try a bigger model, such as OLLAMA_MODEL=qwen2.5:1.5b.
